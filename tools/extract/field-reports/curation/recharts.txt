// Recharts — notes written after reading each issue. Format: see build.mjs.

#263 | chart | all charts
S: With direction: rtl on the page, chart numbers and texts were unreadable and the tooltip opened in the wrong place. Reported in 2016 and closed three years later.
L: Charts are isolated from inherited direction: the SVG root sets its own direction and text anchoring explicitly, and tooltip coordinates are physical.

#682 | chart | BarChart
S: A team supporting Arabic countries used a CSS-flipping plugin around a stacked bar chart and found that the tooltip, the y-axis label and the order of bars were not mirrored — a chart drawn from computed coordinates is not affected by flipped CSS. Closed after two and a half years.
L: Chart mirroring is a data-level option (reverse the category scale, move the value axis to inline-start side, anchor labels), implemented in the layout computation, with direction as an input.

#4214 | keyboard | accessibilityLayer
S: In RTL the chart's keyboard layer moves to the previous point on ArrowLeft and the next on ArrowRight, as in LTR. Open for almost three years.
L: Keyboard traversal of data points follows the visual order of the axis: if the scale is reversed for RTL, the arrow mapping reverses with it.

#7548 | numerals | axis ticks, tooltip
S: Axis ticks, tooltips and labels always used Western digits, which is inconsistent in Arabic and Persian interfaces whose other numbers use local digits.
L: Charts take the locale's number formatter (including the numbering system) for every number they print, by default.
